Executive Summary

BotRent is a production-grade, non-custodial decentralized NFT rental protocol deployed on the Botchain / Bohr Network (Chain ID: 968). BotRent solves liquidity lockup by offering a trustless, smart-contract-enforced escrow marketplace. Token owners deposit ERC-721 digital assets into protocol escrow and define customized rental fees (in native BOT) and durations. Renters obtain verifiable on-chain utility rights without purchasing the underlying digital asset. The protocol enforces automated returns, reentrancy-guarded earnings distribution, and dual-authorization lifecycle terminations with zero administrative backdoors.

1. Problem Statement

  • Capital Lockup & Idle Assets: Over $15B in utility NFTs sit idle in cold storage without risk-free yield mechanisms.
  • High Barrier to Entry: Gamers and Web3 users are priced out of high-value utility assets and metaverse plots.
  • Custody Risks: Traditional rentals require high collateral or insecure peer-to-peer trust models.

2. The BotRent Solution

  • Zero Collateral Required: Renters pay only the duration fee in native BOT.
  • Guaranteed Asset Preservation: Escrow-backed via smart contract; returned directly to the owner.
  • Instant Earnings: Rental revenue credited immediately to owner balance with pull-payment security.
  • Authoritative On-Chain State: Games and dApps query isRentalExpired() and getRental() directly.
